# Screenshot credits

The screenshots in the README show a demo library made only of freely licensed
works. Posters for films without an official one were made by cropping the film's
key art and adding its logo.

## Films and series

Open movies by the Blender Foundation / [Blender Studio](https://studio.blender.org/films/),
licensed under [CC BY](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/):
Sprite Fright, Spring, Sintel, Charge, Tears of Steel, Coffee Run, Wing It!,
Big Buck Bunny, Cosmos Laundromat, Agent 327: Operation Barbershop, Hero,
Elephants Dream and Caminandes.
